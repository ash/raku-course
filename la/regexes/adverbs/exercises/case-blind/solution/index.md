## Codex

```raku
say 'Hello WORLD' ~~ /:i world/;
```

🦋 Inveni codicem fontem in archivo [case-blind.raku](https://github.com/ash/raku-course/blob/master/exercises/regexes/adverbs/case-blind.raku).

## Exitus

```
｢WORLD｣
```

## Commentarii

1. Adverbium `:i` congruentiam differentiam inter litteras maiores et minores neglegere facit.

1. Exemplar minusculum ideo maiusculum `WORLD` congruit.
