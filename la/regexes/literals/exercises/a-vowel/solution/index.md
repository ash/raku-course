## Codex

```raku
say 'Hello' ~~ / <[aeiou]> /;
```

🦋 Inveni codicem fontem in archivo [a-vowel.raku](https://github.com/ash/raku-course/blob/master/exercises/regexes/literals/a-vowel.raku).

## Exitus

```
｢e｣
```

## Commentarii

1. Classis `<[aeiou]>` quinque vocales enumerat; eam congruit quaecumque prima apparet.

1. In `Hello` littera `H` in classe non est, itaque congruentia ab `e` incipit.
