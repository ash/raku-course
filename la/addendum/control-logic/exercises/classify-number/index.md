## Problema

Pro unoquoque numerorum `-4`, `0` et `7`, imprime utrum negativus, nihil,
an positivus sit, et utrum par an impar sit, forma in exemplo ostensa.

## Exemplum

Programma imprimit:

```
-4 is negative and even
0 is zero and even
7 is positive and odd
```
