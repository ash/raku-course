## Problema

Iudica ludos saxi, chartae et forficum. Pro certaminibus
`rock`/`scissors`, `paper`/`rock` et `paper`/`paper`, imprime quis lusor vincat,
vel `tie` cum motus aequant.

## Exemplum

Programma imprimit:

```
rock vs scissors: rock wins
paper vs rock: paper wins
paper vs paper: tie
```
